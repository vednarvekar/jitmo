# Board summary: jitmo

**Vote: 3 FUND IF, 0 fund, 0 pass. Average score 3.3 / 10** (Offers 3, Monopoly 3, Product 4).
Read plainly: the engineering is real and the board would back it, but only if a customer and a single clear promise are chosen first. As it stands today it is a feature list, not a business.

## Risks raised by more than one member (these come first)
1. **No chosen customer, no pain, no price.** All three. Three candidate markets, none tested, no buyer spoken to.
2. **Accuracy is too low for an app that acts.** All three. Router 81-92%, extractor about 63%, chains about 60%, all measured on the dev PC and not the 8 GB laptop. One wrong action in three destroys trust, and a wrong delete or move costs more than a wrong answer.
3. **Competing with bundled and free Windows Copilot** with a feature list. Offers and Monopoly both: a free tier plus a paid tier "later" leaves price as the only difference, and price loses to Microsoft.
4. **Install and distribution are blank.** Monopoly and Product, Offers also asks. Code signing is unresolved, so a first launch shows SmartScreen warnings, and no channel exists for the first 100 users.

## Where they disagree
- **Free tier.** Offers says drop it until a paying buyer exists. Monopoly and Product do not address it; the brief called it the way to spread installs.
- **What the one thing should be.** Monopoly names the privacy-restricted segment (law, finance, clinics) as the only place the local-first claim has teeth. Product wants one flawless command and every other skill cut. Offers wants the market chosen first and the outcome stated with a time attached.
- **Strongest asset.** Offers: cheap delivery makes a generous guarantee affordable. Monopoly: local efficiency is a genuine, checkable edge. Product: craft (14 MB idle, instant feel, safety built in).

## Conditions (a checklist the rest of the pack will test)
- [ ] Pick one customer and one sentence, and cut every skill not needed to deliver it. *(all three)*
- [ ] Talk to at least a handful of real buyers in that group and write down their words. *(Offers, Monopoly)* → `/founder-consumer`
- [ ] Choose the segment where local-first matters; the privacy-restricted one fits best. *(Monopoly)* → `/founder-competitors`
- [ ] State the outcome in one sentence with a time, and test it as a pre-sale or paid pilot at a real price. *(Offers)* → `/founder-pricing`, `/founder-offer`
- [ ] Reach near-perfect accuracy on the chosen skills, measured on the 8 GB laptop, before adding any skill. Hit a stated bar on destructive actions with confirm and Undo proven. *(all three)*
- [ ] Show a measured, repeatable win against Copilot on a defined task set. *(Monopoly)* → `/founder-competitors`
- [ ] Put five real users in front of it and watch the first five minutes unaided. *(Product)*
- [ ] Fix install: pick a code-signing route so first launch is clean. *(Product, Monopoly, Offers)* → `/founder-ops`
- [ ] Name a concrete channel for the first 100 users. *(Monopoly)* → `/founder-marketing`
- [ ] Make failure honest: when confidence is low, ask and show what it understood; never act. *(Product)*
- [ ] Drop the free tier until a paid buyer exists; build a guarantee the numbers can afford. *(Offers)* → `/founder-cfo`
- [ ] At least one buyer commits to a real price. *(Monopoly)*

## The strongest version the board can see
A local, fast, safe desktop assistant sold to **one narrow group that cannot use cloud AI** (a firm in law, finance or healthcare, or a small accounting practice) for **a few repeated tasks it does almost perfectly**, installed cleanly and proven on a modest laptop. That differs from the pitch: the pitch is a general assistant for everyone with a free tier. The board's version is smaller, aimed at buyers who have a hard reason to choose local, and priced from the first customer.

## Open questions nobody can answer yet (and which skill does)
Market size and willingness to pay (`/founder-consumer`, `/founder-pricing`); who else sells to this group (`/founder-competitors`); unit cost and break-even (`/founder-cfo`); cost and route for code signing and install (`/founder-ops`).
