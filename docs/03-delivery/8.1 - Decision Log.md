Effect on open decisions (recommendations, not answers):

- **D4 shared vs separate models.** SP1 shows a small encoder works for routing. SP2 shows extraction needs its own model (GLiNER-class, fine-tuned). Recommend two models: encoder for routing, GLiNER-small int8 for extraction. Update 06 and 07 to match 6.1.
- **D5 model picks.** Encoder: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 int8 works for SP1 (bge-small is the alternative). Extractor: GLiNER-small, fine-tune it. Planner: Qwen2.5-1.5B Q4 is too slow and too inaccurate as it stands; try the 3B, a top-5 skill catalog, or fixed templates.
- **D7 Tauri.** Fits only if the web view is destroyed when idle, or the idle budget is raised. Make the hotkey configurable (Ctrl+Alt+Space was already taken).
- **D11 confidence thresholds.** From SP1: 0.8 or more runs directly (71% of requests, 96% correct); 0.5-0.8 asks to confirm; below 0.5 goes to the planner. Recalibrate on real logs. Doc 07 and 00.3 disagree today and should adopt this.
- **D19 Spotify and DND.** Spotify URI play fails too often, so add a UI Automation Play fallback and test with a Free account. DND toggle works, with visual confirmation still needed.
- **D20 targets.** PR2 (peak under 2GB) is at risk because of the planner alone. PR1 (idle under 300MB) holds only with the web view destroyed. PR4 looks fine for Tier 0.
- **D9 team size.** Docs 8, 10, 11 and 12 assume a team of 6-8. With one person, the schedule needs recalculating.
